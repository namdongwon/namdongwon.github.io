Privacy policy

This is a personal site run by Dongwon Nam. Cookies are used only if you agree to visitor statistics, and Dongwon Nam stores nothing that identifies you (section 4). Information is received only when you choose to leave a note or message, as described below.

1. What is received and why

  • Guestbook — only your note, used only to choose notes for the guestbook. One published note may also be shown as a single line on the book page. When you send, Google Forms also records the time, and Google may process connection details (such as the IP address) under its own privacy policy.
  • Email or Instagram message — your name, email and message are used only to reply.
  • The guestbook does not ask for an email address, phone number or other contact details.

2. How long it is kept

  • Guestbook notes that are not published are deleted within 30 days of receipt.
  • Published notes stay on the site and are removed as soon as you ask.
  • Emails and Instagram messages are deleted within six months of a reply.

3. Transfer outside Korea

  • Recipient: Google LLC (United States and elsewhere) — your note is stored in Dongwon Nam’s Google Form and also copied to the Google Sheet linked to it.
  • What is transferred: the guestbook information listed in section 1.
  • When and how: when you press send, over the internet.
  • Purpose and retention at the recipient: kept in the Google Form and the Google Sheet linked to it; notes that are not published are deleted from both within 30 days of receipt.
  • How to refuse: if you don’t press Send, nothing is sent. You can still reach Dongwon Nam by Instagram message or email.
  • When visitor statistics are produced (only if you agree) — Recipient: Google LLC (United States · Google Analytics). What is transferred: a cookie identifier, the page viewed, the referring site, any click on a link to Kyobo Book Centre, what you type into the site search with the number of results, and whether you reached the end of the poem “Rubbing” (search terms that look like an email address or phone number are not sent), browser and device details, and the connection details your browser sends when a page opens (IP address — used to estimate country and city; Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses). When and how: after you agree, when a page opens, a link to Kyobo Book Centre is clicked, the site search is used or the end of “Rubbing” is reached, over the internet. Purpose and retention: visitor statistics — the statistics are deleted after 14 months. How to refuse: choose ‘Continue without agreeing’ in the consent window, or withdraw at any time under ‘Cookie settings’ at the bottom of each page.
  • When visits are counted — Recipient: Cloudflare, Inc. (United States · Cloudflare Web Analytics). What is transferred: the connection details your browser sends when a page opens (IP address, browser information), plus the page address, where you came from and how fast the page loads. When and how: when a page opens, over the internet. Purpose: counting visits — no cookies or device storage are used, and visits are only added up as totals. How to refuse: turn on your browser’s tracking protection or an ad blocker, or turn off JavaScript, and nothing is counted.

4. Information stored automatically

  • Only if you agree to visitor statistics, two Google Analytics cookies (_ga and one starting with _ga_) are stored under this site’s address. They are used to tell your device apart, expire after two years, and are deleted if you withdraw your consent. The page viewed, referring site, clicks on links to Kyobo Book Centre, site search terms, reaching the end of “Rubbing”, browser, device, country and city (estimated from the IP address), language and screen size are looked at only as totals, and no name or contact details are collected (section 3).
  • Seven small things are kept in this browser’s storage — whether you have already seen the opening door scene, your display setting (system, light or dark), whether you turned on Reduce motion, your text size, a short fingerprint of what each page listed when you last saw it (for the “new” dot), your cookie consent choice, and a guestbook note you started but haven’t sent (removed once sent or after 7 days). They stay on this device, are never sent to Dongwon Nam, and can be removed by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, United States), which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Dongwon Nam does not receive or see these logs.
  • Visits are counted without cookies through Cloudflare Web Analytics. Pages viewed, where visitors came from, browser, operating system and page speed are seen only as totals, and nothing is kept that singles out one person (section 3).

5. Your requests

  • You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the use of anything you have left. Please get in touch by Instagram message or email (see the contact section). The person responsible for personal information is Dongwon Nam.

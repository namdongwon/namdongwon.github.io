Privacy policy

This is a personal site run by Dongwon Nam. Visiting it collects no personal information, and it uses no cookies. Information is received only when you choose to leave a note or message, as described below.

1. What is received and why

  • Guestbook — your name (optional) and note, used only to choose notes for the guestbook. When you send, Formspree also records the time and connection details (such as the IP address).
  • Contact form (once connected) — your name (optional), email and message, used only to reply.
  • The guestbook does not ask for an email address, phone number or other contact details.

2. How long it is kept

  • Guestbook notes that are not published are deleted within 30 days of receipt.
  • Published notes stay on the site and are removed as soon as you ask.
  • Contact messages are deleted within six months of my reply.

3. Transfer outside Korea

  • Recipient: Formspree (United States) — your note passes through this service on its way to Dongwon Nam’s mailbox.
  • What is transferred: the information listed in section 1.
  • When and how: when you press send, over the internet.
  • Purpose and retention at the recipient: forwarding to the mailbox; kept in Formspree’s inbox for 30 days.
  • How to refuse: if you don’t tick the consent box, nothing is sent. You can still reach me by Instagram message or email.

4. Information stored automatically

  • No cookies are used.
  • Two small settings are kept in this browser’s storage — whether you have already seen the opening door scene, and your chosen display (light, dark or system). They stay on this device, are never sent to me, and can be removed by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

5. Your requests

  • You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the use of anything you have left. Please write via the Instagram message or email in the contact section. The person responsible for personal information is Dongwon Nam.

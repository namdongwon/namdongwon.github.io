Privacy policy

This is a personal site run by Dongwon Nam. Dongwon Nam collects no personal information from your visit, and the site uses no cookies. Information is received only when you choose to leave a note or message, as described below.

1. What is received and why

  • Email or Instagram message — your name, email and message are used only to reply.

2. How long it is kept

  • Emails and Instagram messages are deleted within six months of a reply.

3. Transfer outside Korea

  • Dongwon Nam currently receives no information through this site, so none is transferred outside Korea. Emails and Instagram messages are kept by those services.

4. Information stored automatically

  • No cookies are used.
  • Two small settings are kept in this browser’s storage — whether you have already seen the opening door scene, and your display setting (system, light or dark). They stay on this device, are never sent to Dongwon Nam, and can be removed by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, United States), which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Dongwon Nam does not receive or see these logs.

5. Your requests

  • You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the use of anything you have left. Please get in touch by Instagram message or email (see the contact section). The person responsible for personal information is Dongwon Nam.

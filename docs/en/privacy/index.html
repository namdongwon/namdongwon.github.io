1. What is received and why

  • Guestbook — only your note, used only to choose notes for the guestbook. One published note may also be shown as a single line on the book page. When you send, Google Forms also records the time, and Google may process connection details (such as the IP address) under its own privacy policy.
  • Email or Instagram message — your name, email and message are used only to reply.
  • The guestbook does not ask for an email address, phone number or other contact details.
  • Published notes can be read by anyone. Please don’t include anything you would not want others to know, such as health details or contact information.
  • Dongwon Nam does not sell or hand over personal information to any other person or company (no provision to third parties). Service providers are listed in section 6, and transfers outside Korea in section 3.

2. How long it is kept

  • Guestbook notes that are not published are deleted within 30 days of receipt.
  • Published notes stay on the site and are removed as soon as you ask.
  • Emails and Instagram messages are deleted within six months of a reply.
  • How it is deleted — unpublished notes are removed by an automatic deletion set up on the Google Sheet once they are 30 days old, and are also deleted from the Google Form responses.
  • If you ask for a published note to be removed, it is deleted from the site’s source and the site is published again. Because the site’s source is kept in a public repository (GitHub), the note may remain in its history; if you wish, it will be removed from that history as well. Copies already made elsewhere, such as search engine caches, cannot be deleted by Dongwon Nam.
  • Emails and Instagram messages are deleted conversation by conversation (emails also from the trash). No personal information has to be kept under any law.

3. Transfer outside Korea

  • Recipient: Google LLC (United States; data may be stored in the various countries where Google has data centres) — your note is stored in Dongwon Nam’s Google Form and also copied to the Google Sheet linked to it.
  • Recipient’s contact: Google privacy enquiries — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Legal basis: your separate consent given in the transfer checkbox on the guestbook form (Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, Article 28-8(1)(1)). Without it, the note is not sent.
  • What is transferred: the guestbook information listed in section 1.
  • When and how: when you press send, over the internet.
  • Purpose and retention at the recipient: kept in the Google Form and the Google Sheet linked to it; notes that are not published are deleted from both within 30 days of receipt.
  • How to refuse: if you don’t press Send, nothing is sent. You can still reach Dongwon Nam by Instagram message or email. Not sending anything makes no difference to how you can use the site.
  • When visitor statistics are produced (only if you agree) — Recipient: Google LLC (United States · Google Analytics). What is transferred: a cookie identifier, the page viewed, the referring site, any click on a link to Kyobo Book Centre, what you type into the site search with the number of results, and whether you reached the end of the poem “Rubbing” (search terms that look like an email address or phone number are not sent), browser and device details, and the connection details your browser sends when a page opens (IP address — used to estimate country and city; Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses). When and how: after you agree, when a page opens, a link to Kyobo Book Centre is clicked, the site search is used or the end of “Rubbing” is reached, over the internet. Purpose and retention: visitor statistics — the statistics are deleted after 14 months. How to refuse: choose ‘Continue without agreeing’ in the consent window, or withdraw at any time under ‘Cookie settings’ at the bottom of each page. Legal basis: your consent in the consent dialog (Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, Article 28-8(1)(1)). Recipient’s contact: policies.google.com/privacy. Withdrawing consent makes no difference to how you can use the site.
  • When visits are counted — Recipient: Cloudflare, Inc. (United States · Cloudflare Web Analytics). What is transferred: the connection details your browser sends when a page opens (IP address, browser information), plus the page address, where you came from and how fast the page loads. When and how: when a page opens, over the internet. Purpose: counting visits — no cookies or device storage are used, and visits are only added up as totals. How to refuse: turn on your browser’s tracking protection or an ad blocker, or turn off JavaScript, and nothing is counted. Legal basis: visit counting is entrusted to Cloudflare (outsourcing, section 6), and this is disclosed in this policy (Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, Article 28-8(1)(3)). Recipient’s contact: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. Blocking it makes no difference to how you can use the site.

4. Information stored automatically

  • Only if you agree to visitor statistics, two Google Analytics cookies (_ga and one starting with _ga_) are stored under this site’s address. They are used to tell your device apart, expire after two years (Chrome shortens this to 400 days), and are deleted if you withdraw your consent. The page viewed, referring site, clicks on links to Kyobo Book Centre, site search terms, reaching the end of “Rubbing”, browser, device, country and city (estimated from the IP address), language and screen size are looked at only as totals, and no name or contact details are collected (section 3).
  • Eleven small things are kept in this browser’s storage — whether you have already seen the opening door scene, your display setting (system, light or dark), whether you turned on Reduce motion, your text size, a short fingerprint of what each page listed when you last saw it (for the “new” dot), your cookie consent choice, your five most recent site searches (you can clear them in the search box), whether the other-language notice has already been shown, where you clicked or tapped when moving to another page (this tab only, removed as soon as the next page opens), a guestbook note you started but haven’t sent (removed once sent or after 7 days), and the guestbook note you sent (shown back to you until it is published; the text is removed once it is published or after 30 days, leaving only a short fingerprint to mark it as yours). They stay on this device, are never sent to Dongwon Nam, and can be removed by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, United States), which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Dongwon Nam does not receive or see these logs.
  • Visits are counted without cookies through Cloudflare Web Analytics. Pages viewed, where visitors came from, browser, operating system and page speed are seen only as totals, and nothing is kept that singles out one person (section 3).

5. Your requests

  • You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the use of (suspend the processing of) anything you have left. Please write to the email address in section 8 or send an Instagram message (see the contact section).
  • You can withdraw your consent to visitor statistics at any time under ‘Cookie settings’ at the bottom of each page.
  • The legal guardian of a child under 14 may make these requests in the same way.
  • Requests are answered and handled within 10 days. Making a request will never put you at any disadvantage.
  • If you need help with a privacy violation, you can contact the Korean authorities: Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr · 118), Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr · 1833-6972), Supreme Prosecutors’ Office (spo.go.kr · 1301) or Korean National Police Agency (ecrm.police.go.kr · 182).

6. Service providers (outsourcing)

  • Google LLC — receiving and storing guestbook notes (Google Forms · Google Sheets)
  • Google LLC — receiving and storing email (Gmail)
  • Google LLC — visitor statistics (Google Analytics, only with your consent)
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — counting visits (Cloudflare Web Analytics)
  • GitHub, Inc. — hosting the site (GitHub Pages)
  • Any change of provider or task will be reflected in this policy without delay.

7. How information is kept safe

  • The only person who handles personal information is the site’s operator, Dongwon Nam.
  • The site and its admin screen are served only over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • The admin screen is protected by a password and two-step verification (OTP), repeated failed attempts are blocked, and sign-ins are logged and reviewed.
  • The admin screen only reads the Google Sheet holding guestbook notes and does not keep its own copy of them.

8. Person responsible for personal information

  • Dongwon Nam (operator) · email namdonwon@gmail.com — please send any questions, requests or complaints about personal information to this address.

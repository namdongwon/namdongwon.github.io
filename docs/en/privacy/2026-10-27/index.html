1. What is received and why

  • Guestbook — your name (optional) and note, used only to choose notes for the guestbook. When you send, Formspree also records the time and connection details (such as the IP address).
  • Email or Instagram message — your name, email and message are used only to reply.
  • The guestbook does not ask for an email address, phone number or other contact details.

2. How long it is kept

  • Guestbook notes that are not published are deleted within 30 days of receipt.
  • Published notes stay on the site and are removed as soon as you ask.
  • Emails and Instagram messages are deleted within six months of a reply.

3. Transfer outside Korea

  • Recipient: Formspree (United States and elsewhere) — your note passes through this service on its way to Dongwon Nam’s mailbox.
  • What is transferred: the guestbook information listed in section 1.
  • When and how: when you press send, over the internet.
  • Purpose and retention at the recipient: forwarding to the mailbox; kept in Formspree’s inbox for 30 days.
  • How to refuse: if you don’t tick the consent box, nothing is sent. You can still reach Dongwon Nam by Instagram message or email.
  • When visitor statistics are produced (only if you agree) — Recipient: Google LLC (United States · Google Analytics). What is transferred: a cookie identifier, the page viewed, the referring site and any click on a link to Kyobo Book Centre, browser and device details, and the connection details your browser sends when a page opens (IP address — used to estimate country and city; Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses). When and how: after you agree, when a page opens or a link to Kyobo Book Centre is clicked, over the internet. Purpose and retention: visitor statistics — the statistics are deleted after 14 months. How to refuse: choose ‘Continue without agreeing’ in the consent window, or withdraw at any time under ‘Cookie settings’ at the bottom of each page.

4. Information stored automatically

  • Only if you agree to visitor statistics, two Google Analytics cookies (_ga and one starting with _ga_) are stored under this site’s address. They are used to tell your device apart, expire after two years, and are deleted if you withdraw your consent. The page viewed, referring site, clicks on links to Kyobo Book Centre, browser, device, country and city (estimated from the IP address), language and screen size are looked at only as totals, and no name or contact details are collected (section 3).
  • Three small settings are kept in this browser’s storage — whether you have already seen the opening door scene, your display setting (system, light or dark), and your cookie consent choice. They stay on this device, are never sent to Dongwon Nam, and can be removed by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, United States), which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Dongwon Nam does not receive or see these logs.

5. Your requests

  • You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the use of anything you have left. Please get in touch by Instagram message or email (see the contact section). The person responsible for personal information is Dongwon Nam.

Open the door, step out, and it is full of living things
breathing things

Different from things that move
Even dead things can move

It must be dry
It must be far too dry

I touch the wall of a building
Something crumbles off, brittle
like a stamp of black seal ink, without a drop of moisture

It’s old

Trash pushed along by the wind, or
wheels rolling at a steady speed

Should I pretend to be alive
Should I pretend to be dead

I brush the ink off my hand

The wind blows
Now I walk on
